## Brand & Style
The design system drives a high-precision, intense athletic companion built for lifters, strength athletes, and performance coaches. The visual signature merges functional minimalism with an aggressive, technical motorsport-inspired athletic aesthetic. Every screen radiates deliberate energy: high legibility under harsh gym lighting, immediate information retrieval during extreme exertion, and instantaneous interaction feedback.

Key design principles:
- **Kinetic Precision:** High-contrast data readouts with zero decorative fluff. Form always serves speed of execution.
- **Aggressive Ergonomics:** Massive touch targets, tactile affordances, and unmistakable hit states tuned for shaky hands, chalked fingers, and rapid glances.
- **Deep Tonal Immersion:** An abyssal obsidian canvas layered with carbon glass panels and electric neon indicators that command focus without straining eyes in dimly lit training environments.

## Colors
The palette is built upon deep obsidian foundations, high-potency luminescent accents, and zoned functional colors:

- **Primary (`#CCFF00` - Electric Volt):** The core action color. Applied to completion triggers, active set highlights, primary call-to-actions, and positive progress deltas.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF` - Laser Cyan):** Applied to telemetry states, pacing timers, target rep ranges, and technical metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#FF3B30` - Pure Crimson):** Denotes maximal exertion, failure thresholds, RPE 9.5-10 zones, and destructive actions.
- **Warning / Sub-Maximal (`#FF9500` - Kinetic Amber):** Denotes heavy working sets (RPE 8-9) and cautionary status alerts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0C0E12` Base Obsidian):** The deepest viewport floor.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#141820` Carbon Core):** Card containers and standard elevated surfaces.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1C222C` Slate Obsidian):** Inner inset groups, unselected chips, input fields, and elevated modals.
- **Border Ghost (`#2A3241`):** Structural boundaries, calibrated to 1px with 40-70% opacity.
- **Typography High Contrast (`#F4F6F8`):** Maximum legibility for primary metric reads.
- **Typography Muted (`#8E9BAE`):** Secondary metadata, labels, and past-set history.

## Typography
Typography is architected into three discrete functional lanes:

1. **Chivo (Display & Headlines):** Angular, athletic grotesque with authoritative density. Headlines employ uppercase tracking on section leads to project stadium scoreboard impact.
2. **Space Grotesk (Body & Structural Content):** A technical sans offering clean horizontal rhythm, high legibility on mobile screens, and subtle geometric edge.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metrics, Values, Timers & Labels):** Tabular figures enforce identical glyph widths across dynamic countdowns, barbell plate counts, rep records, and volume tallies. It prevents layout jank while sets tick down.

All micro-labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) must be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing for rapid orientation.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a mobile-first fluid columnar framework structured for one-handed thumb interaction within the lower 60% of the screen viewport:

- **Vertical Grid Rhythm:** Enforces a rigid 4px/8px micro-grid. Primary workout cards use `1.25rem` outer margins to guarantee no accidental tap triggers along bezel curves.
- **Hit Zones:** Interactive row targets (e.g., set check-boxes, weight increment steppers) mandate a minimum touch boundary of 56px height.
- **Density Scaling:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Set tables display inline with sticky workout-footer action trays.
  - **Tablet / Large Handheld (>= 768px):** Two-column split with exercise ledger on the left and active barbell telemetry/rest timer pinned on the right.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons traditional soft drop-shadows in favor of luminous depth, obsidian layering, and neon line-radiance:

1. **Canvas Level 0 (`#0C0E12`):** Pure background, completely non-interactive.
2. **Sub-surface Level 1 (`#141820`):** Frosted glass panels using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 85% opacity, paired with an inner top hairline (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Interactive Surface Level 2 (`#1C222C`):** Inputs and inactive set items with a 1px border (`#2A3241`).
4. **Active Luminescence Level 3:** When a card or set is currently engaged, the container introduces a localized glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.18)` and an edge border tinted in Primary Electric Volt (`rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.6)`).

## Shapes
The shape language relies on precision-cut geometry with tightly controlled softness (Level 1 / 4px base radius):

- **Micro elements & inputs:** 4px radius (`rounded-sm`).
- **Cards, modular workout blocks, and action sheets:** 8px radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill-shaped badges & timer capsules:** Continuous circular capsules (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for status chips, intensity tags, and rest countdown widgets.
This maintains a chiseled, industrial aesthetic reminiscent of calibrated iron equipment, steering clear of soft consumer-app roundness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log Set / Finish Workout):** High-voltage Electric Volt background (`#CCFF00`), deep obsidian typography (`#0C0E12`), font weight 700. Min-height 54px. Active state applies a scale transformation to 0.98 with an increased Volt bloom.
- **Secondary (Add Exercise / Modify):** Surface 2 background (`#1C222C`) with a crisp 1px border (`#2A3241`), Laser Cyan text (`#00F0FF`).
- **Destructive / Drop Set:** Bordered with Pure Crimson (`#FF3B30`), high-contrast crimson text on muted dark red translucent fill (`rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.1)`).

### Exercise & Set Cards
- Backed by Carbon Core (`#141820`) glass with 1px border (`#2A3241`).
- Card headers feature uppercase Chivo titles alongside volume summary tags in tabular JetBrains Mono.
- Active set row illuminates with a left vertical border rail (3px solid `#CCFF00`).

### Metric Inputs & Steppers
- Weight and rep fields utilize large monospaced typography (`metric-md`).
- Stepper buttons (+ / -) feature substantial 48x48px touch targets equipped with active haptic rumble feedback.

### Set Completion Checkboxes
- Large 36x36px octagonal or slightly softened squared hit targets. Unchecked state: muted obsidian fill with subtle cyan or border outline. Checked state: immediate Electric Volt solid transition with a deep charcoal checkmark glyph.

### Rest Timer Capsule
- High-visibility floating bar pinned above the bottom bar.
- Laser Cyan progress bar depletion line accompanied by tabular countdown digits (`01:45`).
- Flashes to Electric Volt and fires vibration triggers at 10 seconds remaining.

### Intensity & RPE Badges
- RPE 6–7: Laser Cyan outline (`#00F0FF`) with translucent fill.
- RPE 8–8.5: Kinetic Amber outline (`#FF9500`).
- RPE 9–10: Pure Crimson outline (`#FF3B30`) with pulse animation on personal record attempts.
